# Module 3: Acquisition cost

**Status:** not started.
**Tests PSE objection P-3:** "massive cost associated with buying our system"; Jefferson proponents
estimated $47M and the final cost was $109M (S018).

## Questions

1. What are PSE's Kitsap distribution (and any transmission) assets worth? Compare original cost less
   depreciation (rate base), replacement cost new less depreciation, and the income approach.
2. What are severance/stranded-cost claims, separation and re-integration costs (cutting the Kitsap system
   out of PSE's), and legal and condemnation costs under RCW 54.16.020?
3. How did the Jefferson price move from estimate to closing, and which of those drivers would scale to Kitsap?

## Primary sources to collect

- PSE FERC Form 1 (plant by function, depreciation), PSE UTC rate-case workpapers (rate base by area if available)
- UTC order approving the Jefferson sale; Jefferson PUD audited financials (OQ-11)
- WA condemnation case law for PUD acquisitions

## Rules

- No acquisition figure enters the model without a primary source. The ~$1.2B–$1.5B number is UNVERIFIED (OQ-07).
- A first-order cross-check to run once Jefferson's figures are primary-sourced: cost per customer
  scaled by Kitsap's customer count and asset age. This is a sanity check only, not a valuation.
