# Module 5: Operations

**Status:** not started.
**Tests PSE objections P-4** (no experience at this scale) **and P-5** (storm response: PSE cites 70+
employees and 50+ Potelco contractors including 34 line workers in Kitsap, S018).

## Questions

1. Staffing, line crews, fleet, control center, IT/billing/customer service, and O&M cost per customer,
   benchmarked against Washington utilities of comparable size.
2. Make, buy or contract: in-house crews vs. contract operations vs. joint operating agreements.
3. Storm response and reliability: mutual-aid arrangements (WPUDA, NWPPA), and EIA-861 SAIDI/SAIFI for PSE
   vs. Washington PUDs. The 2025 reliability workbook is already in `data/raw/eia861_2025/`.
4. Transition costs: systems, inventory, training, and the overlap period.

## Benchmarks (EIA-861 2025 customers, S010)

Snohomish PUD 385,159 · PSE 1,251,330 (system) · Mason PUD 3 36,818 · Peninsula Light 35,767 ·
Clallam PUD 34,487 · Jefferson PUD 21,014. A Kitsap PUD (~130,000) would have about 6x Jefferson PUD's
customers and about a third of Snohomish PUD's. The most recent Washington PUD to start electric service,
Jefferson in 2013, did so with about 18,000 customers (S002, S020). That scale gap is the core of P-4 and is
tested here.
