# Module 4: Financing

**Status:** not started.
**Also tests D-2:** voters would grant authority before knowing the cost, with no second vote promised.

## Questions

1. What would revenue bonds cost: tax-exempt rates for a start-up utility with no electric operating
   history, a 25–30 year term, coverage covenants, a debt-service reserve, and capitalized interest
   during transition?
2. What is the annual debt service per customer and per kWh across the module 3 acquisition range?
3. Would a voter-approved bond be required (OQ-16)? KPUD's GM said an acquisition would likely need one
   (S016), but Prop 1 did not approve financing and KPUD promises no second vote (S002 p.2). RCW 54.24.018
   sends some general debt to voters, while its revenue-bond subsection mentions no vote (S026).
   Are federal loan programs available (Jefferson used USDA Rural Utilities Service, S020)?

## Primary sources to collect

- Municipal market data for comparable utility revenue bonds (EMMA official statements: Jefferson PUD,
  other WA PUD issues)
- RUS program eligibility rules
- KPUD's existing debt and credit rating
