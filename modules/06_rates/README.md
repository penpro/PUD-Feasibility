## Questions

   A&G (module 5) + taxes and payments in lieu of taxes − other revenue. What does that imply for rates?
2. Apples-to-apples bills: same usage (for example 800, 1,000, 1,500 kWh/month, plus winter and summer
   profiles), same rate class, PSE's current and filed tariffs vs. the PUD's modeled rates, with sensitivity bands.
3. What do PSE's pending rate increases do to the comparison? KUOW reports a plan to raise rates 30% over
   three years (S016); confirm from the UTC docket.
